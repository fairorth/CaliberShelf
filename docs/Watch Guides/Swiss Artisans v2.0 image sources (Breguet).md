## 6. Chaumet-era / Daniel Roth-era Breguet (1970s–80s revival)

**Image pick — Classique 3137 (moonphase + power reserve + pointer date, the 3130's display-back sibling):**
- **Page URL:** https://www.the1916company.com/blog/a-breguet-classique-3137-with-intricately-hand-engraved-movement-from-the-daniel-roth-era.html
- **Direct images:**
  - Hero: https://watchbox-blog.imgix.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/2025_01_29_AWAW_Breguet-Classique-Power-Reserve-3137BA-11-986_HEADER.jpg
  - Movement: https://watchbox-blog.imgix.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/2025_01_29_AWAW_Breguet-Classique-Power-Reserve-3137BA-11-986_05.jpg
- **Credit:** The 1916 Company (studio photography)
- **What it shows:** Ref **3137BA/11/986** in yellow gold — multi-pattern hand guilloché (straight-line + rose engine), moonphase, power reserve, pointer date; layout drawn from Breguet pocket watch No. 5 (1794). Dial-forward hero plus engraved cal. 502 shot.
- **Second option (3130 proper, pre-A series):** Watch Brothers London, 1987 Classique 3130 "Pre A Series" — https://www.watchbrotherslondon.com/sales-corner/p/evuzly6n3eegnausy5irzifs2ew5ct (off-list, strong photos); also Watches of Knightsbridge — https://watchesofknightsbridge.com/product/breguet-classique-moonphase-power-reserve-3130/

**Reference-pinning notes for the guide (Phillips article, "The Fine Print: Exploring Breguet in the Daniel Roth Era" — https://www.phillips.com/article/154947969/exploring-breguet-in-the-daniel-roth-era — plus Tahoma collectors' guide https://www.tahomawatches.com/read/collectors-guide-to-early-breguet):**
- Perpetual calendars: **3050** (1976, the first major Roth-era release), **3057** (adds exhibition caseback), **3310** (1986, adds power reserve), **3787** (retrograde PC, late 80s–late 90s), **3470/3477** (PC + equation of time), **3617** (PC chronograph, early 90s).
- Power-reserve/moonphase family: **3130** (solid back) / **3137** (display back), 36mm, circa 1990 introduction (3130 earlier — Watch Brothers date a pre-A-series to 1987); Roth-era guilloché throughout.
- Chronographs: **3230/3237** (36mm, Lemania 2310). Tourbillons: **3350** (1988) → **3357** (post-Investcorp), **3450**, **3355** skeleton (1993). Also **3040** "Serpentine" day-date, **3330** "Chinoise", **3637** minute repeater (1993), **3947/5947** rattrapante.
- Production details: 36mm coin-edge (fluted) cases in yellow/white/pink gold are the era's signature (repeaters/chronos 37–40mm); movement bases are the ultra-thin **cal. 502** (F. Piguet 71 derivative) and **Lemania** (2310 for chronos); dials are silvered with engine-turned centres (Clous de Paris, damier croisé, wave) and heat-blued hands. Roth moved production to the Vallée de Joux in 1976; Chaumet bankruptcy → Investcorp acquisition 1987/88, Roth departs 1988, several refs continue into the 1990s.
