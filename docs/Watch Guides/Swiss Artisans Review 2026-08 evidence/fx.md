## Conversion convention (state this in the report)

Foreign-currency comps are converted at the **stamped 2026-08-13 rate**, not the
rate on the sale date. This gives "what that sum is worth in USD today", which is
the right frame for setting a band a buyer will spend against today.

**It is NOT the same as the contemporaneous USD price, and on older CHF results
the gap is material** — the USD has weakened against the CHF since 2021-22:

- P7 Christie's CHF 13,860 (2022, incl. prem.): ≈ **$14,000 contemporaneous** vs
  **$17,052 at today's rate**.
- P7 Christie's CHF 12,500 (2021, incl. prem.): ≈ **$13,000 contemporaneous** vs
  **$15,381 at today's rate**.
- P5 Mercator CHF 44,100 (Christie's 2024-05-13, incl. prem., hammer 35,000):
  ≈ **$50,000 contemporaneous** vs **$54,262 at today's rate**.

Both framings appear in the per-entry tables where they diverge by >10%; the band
uses the today's-rate figure and says so.
