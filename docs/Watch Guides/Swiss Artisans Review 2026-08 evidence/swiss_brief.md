# Swiss Artisans Guide Review — Evidence Collection Brief (2026-08-13)

You are one of five venue-specialist evidence collectors for an adversarial
review of a personal collection guide covering Jaeger-LeCoultre (JLC) and
Vacheron Constantin (VC). Your job is to COLLECT COMPS, not to judge bands.
A separate synthesis stage computes validated bands from the pooled rows.

## Hard rules (violations make your output unusable)

1. **Every number must come from a page you actually searched/fetched THIS
   session.** No prices from model memory — none. If you cannot find data
   for an entry at your venue, write "NO DATA FOUND" for that entry. An
   honest gap is valuable; an invented comp poisons the review.
2. **Every comp row carries: date (listing/sale date, best known) · SOLD or
   ASK tag · price · currency · condition/completeness note (full set?
   polished? redial suspicion? service history?) · source URL.**
3. Prefer evidence from the last 18 months. Older is acceptable for thin
   vintage refs but must show its date.
4. Note when a price includes/excludes buyer's premium or shipping if the
   source says.
5. These are niche references — expect thin data. Do NOT pad thin results
   with lookalike references (e.g., a plain Reverso is NOT a comp for the
   Grande Reverso GMT; a Memovox E861 speedbeat is a DIFFERENT watch from
   the E855; note sister-reference evidence explicitly as such if you
   include it).
